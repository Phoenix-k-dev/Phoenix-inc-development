1. Data controller

The services are operated by Phoenix Inc | Development. For privacy-related requests, contact phoenixinc.dev@gmail.com.

2. Data we may process

Website, contact and quotes

When you contact us or request a quote, we may receive information you voluntarily provide, such as your name or handle, email address, Discord identifier, project type, indicative budget, message content and information required to follow up your request.

Synapse and Discord services

Synapse may process technical Discord identifiers such as server, channel, role, user, message or interaction IDs together with configurations required by enabled modules. The Builder may analyse server structure including categories, channels, roles, permissions, naming conventions, emojis and information needed to adapt the server.

Depending on enabled features, Synapse may retain ticket settings, language preferences, access rules, Interserver links, review/reputation data, Premium status, structure snapshots and related settings.

Scripts, digital products and custom services

For FiveM scripts, tools, applications or custom services, we may process information required for delivery, support, licensing, invoicing or project follow-up depending on the sales or contact channel used.

We aim to process only data reasonably necessary for the requested service, support, security and applicable obligations.

3. Purposes

  • provide, operate and secure Phoenix Inc | Development services;
  • operate Synapse and its modules;
  • analyse and adapt Discord servers when the Builder is used;
  • manage tickets, configuration, Interserver links, Premium and backups;
  • deliver and support scripts and digital products;
  • respond to contact, support and quote requests;
  • prevent abuse, fraud and security incidents;
  • improve features and fix errors;
  • meet applicable contractual, accounting or legal obligations.

4. Legal basis and consent

Depending on the situation, processing may rely on performance of a requested service, your consent, our legitimate interest in securing and improving the services, or compliance with a legal obligation. Where consent is required, it may be withdrawn for the future as provided by applicable law.

5. Retention

Data is retained for a period proportionate to its purpose. Synapse configuration may remain stored while the bot is used on a server or while needed for its functionality. Technical snapshots may be retained for troubleshooting or restoration.

Commercial exchanges, invoices, licence records or support information may be retained longer where required by law or needed as evidence.

6. Providers and recipients

Data may be processed through services needed to operate the project, including Discord, HytHost, GitHub, form-delivery services used by the website and relevant payment or distribution platforms.

These providers have their own privacy policies and may process certain information for their own purposes. We do not sell personal data.

7. Payments and subscriptions

Payments may be processed by third-party platforms such as Discord, including Synapse Premium, or Tebex for selected digital products. Payment-card data is processed by the relevant payment provider and is not intended to be stored directly by Phoenix Inc | Development.

We may nevertheless receive transaction-related information such as order IDs, payment status, the purchased product or subscription, associated server, amount and information needed for support or accounting.

8. Cookies and local storage

The website may use browser local storage to remember functional preferences such as language, theme or interface state. External providers loaded by the website may also use their own cookies or similar technologies.

If non-essential cookies are introduced, the appropriate information or consent mechanism will be implemented where required.

9. Your rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of personal data concerning you and, where applicable, restriction, objection or portability under the relevant law.

To exercise your rights, contact phoenixinc.dev@gmail.com. For Discord-related requests, providing your Discord user ID and the relevant server ID can help us verify the request.

10. Security

We use reasonable safeguards to reduce unauthorized access, including separation of secrets, keeping tokens outside public code, limited Discord permissions, access controls and backups where appropriate. No system can be guaranteed perfectly secure.

11. Children

Services must be used in accordance with the minimum-age rules of the relevant platforms, including Discord, and local law. We do not knowingly seek to collect data from people who do not meet the minimum requirements for the relevant service.

12. Changes

This policy may evolve as products, providers or regulatory requirements change. The date shown at the top identifies the currently published version.
