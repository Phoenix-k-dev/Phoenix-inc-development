Privacy Policy.

This policy explains how Phoenix Inc | Development processes data related to the website, contact forms and the Discord bot Synapse.

1. Data controller

The service is operated by Phoenix Inc | Development. For any privacy request, contact phoenixinc.dev@gmail.com.

2. Data we process

Website and forms

When you submit a quote request or a question, we may receive information you provide voluntarily, such as your name or handle, email address, subject, project type, indicative budget and message content.

Synapse

To operate on Discord, Synapse may process technical Discord identifiers such as server, channel, role, user and message IDs, together with configuration required by enabled modules. The Builder may analyse a server structure, including categories, channels, roles, permissions, naming conventions, emojis and visual conventions required to adapt changes to that server.

Depending on enabled modules, Synapse may store functional data such as ticket settings, reviews/reputation, language preferences, Interserver links, Premium codes, structure snapshots and related settings.

Synapse is not designed to collect private conversation content beyond what is necessary for a feature explicitly used or enabled by the user.

3. Why we use this data

  • to provide and operate Synapse;
  • to analyse and adapt Discord server structures when the Builder is used;
  • to manage tickets, settings, modules, preferences and Interserver links;
  • to respond to support, quote and contact requests;
  • to prevent abuse and secure the service;
  • to improve features and fix errors.

4. Data retention

Data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose. Synapse configuration may remain stored while the bot is used on a server or while required for enabled functionality. Emails and form requests may be retained for support, commercial follow-up and applicable legal obligations.

Structure snapshots created before certain replacement operations may be kept for future troubleshooting or restoration.

5. Service providers and sharing

Data may pass through technical providers needed to operate the project, including Discord for the application and bot, HytHost for bot hosting, GitHub for public source-code hosting and FormSubmit for certain website form deliveries. Payment services such as Discord or Tebex may also process data when paid functionality is offered.

We do not sell personal data.

6. Access, correction and deletion

You may request access to, correction of or deletion of personal data related to you by contacting phoenixinc.dev@gmail.com. For Discord-server data, please provide the server ID and your Discord user ID when possible so we can verify the request.

Some data may be retained where required for legal, security or rights-protection purposes.

7. Security

We use reasonable measures to reduce unauthorized access, including keeping secrets separate from public code, storing tokens outside the public repository and using Discord permission controls. No system can be guaranteed perfectly secure.

8. Children

Synapse and related services must be used in accordance with Discord’s minimum-age requirements and applicable local law. We do not knowingly seek to collect data from children who are not eligible to use Discord.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may change as Synapse and Phoenix Inc | Development services evolve. The update date at the top identifies the current version.
